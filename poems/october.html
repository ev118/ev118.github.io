October

20 October 2026

Come October
all the flowers are faded
the grass is brown
as though it will not be revived
revitalized
imbued with a hue new yet old
old as the trees, ever green,
when the rain starts to fall on deaf ears
and the city turns dreary
come October.

Come October
the colors change in a blur
yellow leaves are turning red
and soon, too soon, will fall
revealing only black wet bark behind
the sky is gray, descending droplets
but the sun breaks through the mist
a fleeting sunset of pink orange red
before it leaves behind but darkness
and the streetlights as our only guidance
in October.

Come December
snow will fall and block the path
the mountains rising stark above the fog
or concealed altogether
and in February
ice and wind will pierce the skin
and pierce the soul
how sharp will be the memory
how soft the rain and grasses
in the autumn but
as April showers bring May flowers
spring will blossom,
breathing life into the trees to make the forest ever green
rushing down the river with the kayaks
ambling then running with the wildlife toward the coastline
bringing back the summer
till the ocean’s roar is heard once more
till the lake reflects the stars dipped in the water
the orange moon
the coming and the going,
from the seeds that once we planted
in October.
